A focused, fully offline counter for pachislot small roles (koyaku).

• Count with the volume keys — assign a role to volume up and down, count without looking at the screen.
• Double-click the same key to undo the last count.
• Works with the screen off (foreground service + MediaSession).
• Haptic feedback: one short tick per count, a double tick for undo.
• Enter the game count to see the hit rate as 1/6.4 and so on.
• Editable counters with colours, key bindings and ordering; Juggler/Hana-Hana/AT presets.
• Session history with counts and probabilities.

No INTERNET permission, no ads, no analytics, no backup. All data stays on the device.
